# Decisions

Format: **D<n> (date) — decision.** Why. Runbook said / alternative considered.

- **D1 (2026-10-29) — Paths: project `/scratch1/pbanga/ee599`, work dirs `/scratch1/pbanga/rcwork` (x265) and `/scratch1/pbanga/rcwork_x264`.** Runbook's `/scratch/$USER` does not exist on CARC (it is `/scratch1`). Scratch has no backups -> code + reports are periodically copied to `/home1/pbanga/ee599_backup`.
- **D2 — Conda env by `--prefix` on scratch, Python 3.12, latest available package versions (recorded in `LEARNINGS.md` once built).** Runbook's pins (Python 3.14.4, torch 2.14.1, numpy 2.5.4, pandas 3.1.0) are unverified; exact pins don't matter because this is a new implementation, not a bit-exact rerun.
- **D3 — `PATH` to x265/x264 set inside job scripts, not `~/.bashrc`; job scripts do `module purge` and never `source ~/.bashrc`.** Cluster policy: batch jobs must not rely on login env; editing `.bashrc` is risky on HPC.
- **D4 — Parallelism across nodes.** Runbook allows one node per script. Since the code is new, encode-heavy stages (infos, hindsight search, evaluation) are sharded over GOPs as SLURM job arrays, and PPO runs one job per (k, seed). Per-job training recipe (RC_ENVS=14, n_steps=64) is unchanged, so this is not a scale-up.
- **D5 — Every source is centre-cropped to 16:9 and scaled to 320x180 (lanczos) at download time, keeping all source frames (no fps change).** CIF is 4:3; cropping avoids aspect distortion. Big sources (1080p, films) are streamed into the downscaler and never stored at full size (saves ~40 GB of scratch).
- **D6 — Data sources (runbook's "12 pilot clips from GitHub" have no stated source, so they are replaced):** 31 Xiph CIF + 11 Xiph 720p + 19 Xiph 1080p, 5 films (BBB, Elephants Dream, Sintel trailer from Xiph y4m masters; Tears of Steel 720p and Sintel 1080p (720p only offered as .zip) from download.blender.org), YouTube UGC 360P originals, first 10 per category (name order) over 13 categories (~125 clips, ~22 GB transfer). Total transfer ≈ 40 GB as approved.
- **D7 — STATIC pattern = offsets (−6, −3, +3) for [I frame, P1–P9, P10–P29].** The runbook gives the three numbers but not the segment boundaries; split at frame 10 is assumed. `static_pattern` study refits pattern and split on training data and reports how far the assumed one is from the fitted one.
- **D8 — Action = per-frame QP offset in {−3..+3} on top of base QP (30 or 38) + STATIC.** Runbook does not state the action set.
- **D9 — Bits per frame are counted from the Annex-B bitstream for both encoders** (VCL NAL units + VPS/SPS/PPS attributed to the next frame; SEI and AUD excluded so x264's version-string SEI doesn't inflate the IDR frame). Runbook used x265's CSV for x265; `codec_check` cross-checks the two.
- **D10 — Quality metric: per-frame 6:1:1 weighted YUV PSNR, GOP PSNR = mean of per-frame values; decoded with ffmpeg.** Runbook says "PSNR" without specifying.
- **D11 — Encoder preset `fast` for all forced-QP and own-RC encodes; `ultrafast` for the first-pass features.** Runbook doesn't name a preset; `fast` keeps the 1M+ search encodes affordable. Single-threaded (`--frame-threads 1 --no-wpp --pools none` / `--threads 1`) for determinism.
- **D12 — GOP windows:** evenly spaced, non-overlapping 30-frame windows per clip: 8 per Xiph/UGC clip, 60–90 per film (skipping the first 2 s and last 10 s of films for titles/credits). Plus **spliced GOPs** (2–3 segments from different sources of the same split, cut frames recorded): 120 train / 40 val / 60 test. They give ground truth for the scene-detector study and make cuts common enough to learn from.
- **D13 — Splits by source:** each Xiph/UGC clip goes to train/val/test by a hash of its name (60/20/20); films are split whole: BBB + Elephants Dream → train, Tears of Steel → val, Sintel (film + trailer) → test. Deterministic, so both encoders get identical GOPs.
- **D14 — Budget and score:** budget = bits of the encoder's *own* `--qp` mode (I frame gets the encoder's default ipratio offset) at QP 30 / 38; the scoring curve is the same mode at QP 18–50. Penalised score = saving − 2 × (% over budget); % GOPs over budget always reported. The runbook says "penalised" without the weight; 2 makes 1 % overspend cost more than the ~1 % saving it can buy.
- **D15 — Offline data cap:** per (GOP, budget) the 24 best + 24 random logged search plans become CQL/BC/IQL trajectories (runbook's `LOG_CAP` idea, applied to every k so the k curve is not confounded by data size).
- **D16 — Restructured pipeline.** The runbook's 13 historical steps (rl_suggestions, rl_matrix, seeds, switch, overshoot, lagr, cutspec, expand, extend, expand2) were the laptop project's chronological history; here they become phases: `infos` → `scenes` → `search` → `baselines` → `rl` (one job per run) → `analysis` → `codecs`. Every runbook study keeps an equivalent: switch, overshoot, lagr/PID-lagr, cutspec, static_pattern, own RC, k curve; `expand2_report.md` and `codecs_report.md` are still produced.
- **D17 — Strict lookahead.** At lookahead k the observation contains only frames t..t+k; all summary features (window cost mean, scene share, distance to next cut) are computed over visible frames only. Frame position and the STATIC offsets of unseen frames are known (they are not lookahead).
- **D18 — Rules as lookahead baselines.** Besides the runbook's causal rule, a deterministic *lookahead rule* (cost-share allocation over the visible window) is evaluated at every k, so the RL k curve has a non-learned counterpart; a *scene rule* adds extra bits on detected cut frames.
- **D19 — MPC (model-based) method:** per-frame bits/PSNR MLP trained on search logs + CEM receding-horizon planning; maximises predicted mean PSNR under the budget (never uses the constant-QP curve, which a causal controller cannot know).
- **D20 — MPC plans against a safety margin.** With full information the bits model is unbiased (actual/predicted GOP bits: median 1.03, p10–p90 0.88–1.19), yet v1 MPC was over budget on 60–80 % of GOPs: CEM pushes PSNR right up to the predicted budget and selects plans the model underestimates. v2 plans against `margin × budget`, margin ∈ {0.80 … 1.0} chosen per run on val (sweep stored in `eval.pkl["margin_sweep"]`). v1 results kept in `results_rl_v1/`.
- **D21 — DQN update rate.** v1 used SB3 `train_freq=4` with 14 vec-envs → ~1 000 gradient steps for 60 k transitions (and the score fell as ε decayed). v2: `train_freq=1, gradient_steps=N_ENVS` (one gradient step per transition), lr 1e-4, exploration_fraction 0.5. Env-step budget unchanged (60 k) so the method comparison stays equal-data.
- **D22 — Common-yardstick scores skip extrapolation.** In `codecs.py`, x264 points whose PSNR lies outside x265's QP 18–50 curve are left out of "score common"/"saving common" (counted in an `out of ref range` column); log-bits extrapolation there gave −1379 / −3e11.
- **D23 — Extensions matching the external extend_report.md.** (a) `experiments/breakdown.py`: no new encodes; per-group results (scene cut / natural / spliced / stable / gradual), their headline metric (saving clipped ±50 %, >1 % over) next to ours, bootstrap CIs, win/loss analysis vs the k=5 lookahead rule. (b) New runs (`runs/extend.txt`, 28 per encoder): `--extra fb` (achieved PSNR last/mean, bit-prediction error last/mean, frames since cut; online env decodes every step), `--extra sum` (fb + window summary replacing the raw lookahead slots) at k=5 and k=29; `cql_a2c` (A2C warm-started from CQL like CQL->PPO); `cql_dqn` (DQN Q-net initialised from the CQL Q-function, ε 0.1→0.02); `ensemble` (mean action probabilities of the 3 CQL->PPO k=5 seeds, no training); `gate` (per-frame PPO gate on the k=0 observation choosing the k=0 or k=5 CQL->PPO policy, charged λ ∈ {1, 3} score points per GOP fully at k=5); `lagr_ft`/`pid_ft` (continue the k=5 CQL->PPO policy 40 k steps under the Lagrangian/PID reward with cut GOPs sampled 4×). gate / *_ft / ensemble depend on the finished CQL->PPO k0/k5 runs.
- **D24 — MPC margin grid 0.60–1.0 (step 0.05).** Every x265 v2 MPC run chose 0.80, the lowest margin offered, so the optimum lay outside the grid. v2 results kept in results_rl_v2/.
- **D25 — Multi-GOP rate control (#2, `rclib/clipenv.py`, `experiments/clip_rl.py`).** Episode = 8 IDR GOPs (same-clip time-ordered runs, or mixed streams from different clips) under one budget = s × bits at constant QP q0 (train: q0 ∈ 24–44, s ∈ U(0.8, 1.25); eval: q0 ∈ {30, 38}, s ∈ {0.85, 1.0, 1.18}), so the QP that meets the budget is unknown. Each step picks one GOP's constant QP as an offset −6..+6 on a causal model QP (first-pass model, 5.5 QP per doubling fitted on train, online calibration). GOP outcomes come from the real constant-QP curves in infos, so there are no encodes; frame-level control is not included. Stream PSNR is pooled over MSE: with a mean of dB values the hindsight plan reached +36 % by putting bits into cheap static GOPs. Baselines: hand rule (offset 0), best constant QP in hindsight, and the hindsight per-GOP plan (Lagrangian sweep + greedy). The hindsight plan often runs below budget, because the score (saving at equal PSNR) does not reward using the budget, and its offsets saturate at +6. Methods: BC (imitates the hindsight plan), PPO from scratch (2 M steps), BC→PPO; lookahead L ∈ {0, 2} GOPs; 3 seeds.
- **D26 — Model-based pre-training (#3, `rclib/mbenv.py`, methods `mb`/`mb_ppo`).** `mb` = CQL distillation, then 1 M PPO steps inside the learned per-frame model from the k=5 MPC run of the same seed. `mb_ppo` = `mb`, then 40 k PPO steps on real encodes. Compare with CQL→PPO (60 k real steps). The model is used only for differences from the GOP's real STATIC encode. Scoring the model's raw PSNR on the real curve was unusable (mean error −196 points). With anchoring, on val the model scores a CQL→PPO policy at 11.3 vs 12.9 real (corr 0.60, MAE 13.7 points per GOP). The injected noise (GOP bias sd 0.10, frame sd 0.05 in log2 bits) is kept small because the model's own error already acts as noise. k ∈ {0, 5}, 3 seeds. **Outcome (v2 guards, 2026-11-02):** stable; learns budget safety but loses saving; mb_ppo below CQL→PPO on x265 k=5, on par or better on x264 and at k=0; Lagrangian still best (see LEARNINGS).
- **D27 — Constrained RL vs imitation (#1, `experiments/constraint.py`).** No new runs. BC vs CQL→PPO vs Lagrangian/PID (from the offline start, and as fine-tunes) vs the ensemble at k = 0/5/10, with overshoot statistics and paired bootstrap CIs. Reports in `results/<codec>/constraint_report.md`.
- **D28 — Wide-budget study (2026-11-03).** Budgets at QP 26, 30, 34, 38, 42 instead of 30/38, so results span a 4x bitrate range. The policy's budget input becomes continuous: `C.budget_level(b)` = (b − min) / (max − min), which equals the old 0/1 flag when the budgets are 30/38, so the two-budget runs are unchanged. Per-frame cost features stay normalised by the QP-30 budget (`C.BUDGET_REF`). New work dirs `rcwork_wide` / `rcwork_x264_wide` link to the source clips/gops. `experiments/widen.py` adds STATIC encodes and the hindsight search only at 26/34/42 and merges them with the source 30/38 results. Reuse is exact because a budget's search seeds are 1000·s + b. Retrained (runs/wide.txt, 69 runs): the CQL→PPO k sweep, plus BC, Lagrangian and PID at k = 0, 5, 10, 3 seeds each. Same steps per run; with 5 budgets each budget gets ~2/5 of the training episodes. Rules, own RC and spend curve are recomputed at 5 budgets. Reports go to `results/<codec>_wide/`, including the new `budgets_report.md` (per-budget tables, per-budget lookahead share, paired CIs). Max plan QP is 42+6 = 48 and min is 26−9 = 17, both near the 18–50 curve range, so the budgets stop at 26/42.
- **D29 — Fair head-to-head with the encoders' own rate control (2026-11-04, `experiments/own_rc_stream.py`).** On the D25 multi-GOP test streams (no oracle QP for anyone: each controller gets only the stream's bit budget), x265/x264 encode each 8-GOP stream as one 240-frame encode (IDR every 30, closed GOPs, `--repeat-headers` on x265) at the budget as an average bitrate; kbps is an integer, so the frame rate is set to make kbps/fps equal budget/240 exactly. Configs: ABR (AQ/cutree off, as the curves), ABR + cutree at rc-lookahead 30 / 90 (matching RL L=0 / L=2), two-pass, and ABR / two-pass with the encoder defaults (AQ + cutree). Debug check: the stream encode at constant QP reproduces the per-GOP curve bits exactly (x265) / within 0.3 % (x264), and ABR without cutree gives identical bitstreams at rc-lookahead 30 and 90 (it does not use the lookahead), hence the cutree configs. CIs: cluster bootstrap over source clips (same-clip streams) and streams (mixed). Remaining bias favours the encoder: it picks a QP per frame, RL one per GOP.
- **D30 — Validity fixes (2026-11-04).** (1) `experiments/reanalysis.py`: every headline claim re-tested with clip-level cluster bootstrap, natural (non-spliced) GOPs only, and per seed; no new encodes. (2) BC + feedback control (`policies.Feedback`, `experiments/bc_feedback.py`): finished BC policies plus a closed-loop budget correction (gain × log2 of spend vs the expected spend curve, margin), (gain, margin) tuned on val; the non-RL answer to "is RL needed for the budget?". (3) Causal per-GOP env (`RC_CAUSAL=1`, work dirs `rcwork_wide_causal` / `rcwork_x264_wide_causal`, symlinked infos/search/scenes): budget = s × constant-QP bits at b with s unknown (train U(0.8, 1.25), eval one of 0.85/1.0/1.18 per case by hash); base QP from a first-pass STATIC rate model fitted on train (`experiments/causal_prep.py`, `causal_prior.json`) instead of the oracle b; per-frame cost features normalised by B/30; cut/flash/fade flags recomputed from visible frames only. Offline search plans are re-expressed as offsets on the model base QP (plans leaving −3..3 are dropped). Own RC is rerun on the same scaled budgets. MPC / mb / gate are not ported.
- **D31 — Move to dataset v1 in stages, residual RL on the causal controller (2026-11-05, user decision).** Work dirs `/scratch1/pbanga/rcwork_v1/<codec>`, reports `results/v1/<codec>`, never mixed with the initial dataset. Stage 1: BC, CQL→PPO, Lagrangian, PID-Lagrangian at k = 0, 5, both encoders. Stage 2: full sweep (k = 0, 1, 2, 5, 10, 29; 5 seeds) on the best of those. Stage 3: cheap extras that reuse policies (cut-triggered lookahead, ensembles). Other learners are not rerun. Why residual: a ±3 action on a fixed base QP cannot reach the budget at k = 0, where the base-QP estimate is off by 3.6–4 QP; the online controller stays over budget in under 5 % of runs and makes "does RL add anything over it?" a direct test. Alternative rejected: action range ±8. Success criterion fixed before the runs: a difference counts only if it is at least 1 point, its clip-level CI excludes 0 and all seeds agree.
- **D32 — Learning curve before any dataset expansion (2026-11-05, user decision).** `rl.py --train-frac` trains on a share of whole training clips (per-seed nested subsets), evaluates val, test and 120 of the trained GOPs, and writes to `results_rl_lc`, apart from `results_rl`. First run: CQL→PPO, causal x265, five budgets, 12.5/25/50/100%, k=0 and 5, 3 seeds (`runs/learning_curve.txt`, `tools/submit_lc.sh`), on the initial dataset because v1 search logs do not exist yet; repeat on v1 after Stage 1, reported separately.

## D33 (2026-11-10): penalise unused budget
Problem: score = saving − 2 × % over had no cost for unused budget; learners spent 78–96% of the budget and delivered 0.3–1.2 dB less PSNR than STATIC (teammate reported the same in their setup).
Decision: score −= RC_UNDER_PENALTY × max(0, % unused − RC_UNDER_TOL), with penalty 1 point per % and tolerance 3%. Applies to the search, offline rewards (via search logs), the relative reward, and the Lagrangian objective (overshoot stays the constraint). Default 0 keeps all earlier runs reproducible.
First use: v1 Stage 1 rerun (`tools/submit_v1u.sh`, work dirs `rcwork_v1/<codec>_u`, reports `results/v1u/<codec>`); infos/scenes/controller plans reused via `experiments/v1_rescore.py` (no re-encoding). Stage 2/3 held until this reward is confirmed. Weight and tolerance may change to match the teammate's fix.
- **D34 — Stream-level RL on dataset v1, scored in dB at the budget (2026-11-11, `rclib/streamenv.py`, `experiments/stream_rl.py`).** After P2's fair redo (no per-GOP learner beats his tuned controller final3), we move RL to the decision a per-GOP rule cannot make: how to share ONE budget across the GOPs of a stream. Episode = 8 IDR GOPs (every window of consecutive GOPs of a v1 clip, plus 100 mixed streams; splices excluded); action = per-GOP constant QP as offset −6..+6 on the causal model QP (D25); outcomes from the real constant-QP curves (no encodes; frame-level control inside the GOP is not modelled). Budget = s × sum of the GOPs' v1 clip-level budgets at q0 ∈ {24, 30, 36, 42}, s ∈ {0.85, 1, 1.18}; train q0 ∈ 24–44, s ∈ U(0.8, 1.25) from the same clip-mean construction (matches budgets.csv to median 0.996); q0 and s are never observed. Score = P2's dB metric on the stream's pooled PSNR: dPSNR_B − 0.17 dB per 1 % over (copied from his metrics.py). Baselines: equal split per GOP (the per-GOP setting of all earlier work) and the remaining-budget rule, each with margin m tuned on val over 0.90–1.04; best constant QP and the hindsight per-GOP plan (offline). Learners: bc, ppo, bc_ppo, bc_ppo_lag (λ = 0.34), L ∈ {0, 2} GOPs, 3 seeds, test read once.
  - Hypotheses, written before any result: **H1** the hindsight plan beats the tuned rule by ≥ 0.3 dB on val (otherwise there is no room and we stop). **H2** bc_ppo at L=2 beats the tuned rule on test with the 95 % CI above 0. **H3** the RL gain over the rule is larger on mixed streams than same-clip ones. **H4** RL's >1 % over rate is within 5 points of the rule's. **H5** the tuned rule beats equal split (stream-level allocation matters at all).
  - Limitation stated up front: per-GOP constant QP, so the frame-level gains of final3 are not included on either side; a full-encode check (RL allocates each GOP's budget, final3 spends it) follows only if H2 holds.
  - Outcome (2026-11-11): H1–H5 all pass on both encoders; see JOURNAL. Next, if pursued: full-encode check where the policy sets each GOP's budget and P2's final3 controller spends it.
- **D35 — Closing the D34 holes (2026-11-11).** (a) Hole 4: the encoders' own stream rate control on the D34 v1 test streams at s = 1, scored with the dB metric (`experiments/own_rc_stream_v1.py`; ABR, ABR+VBV with a 1-s buffer, ABR+cutree, encoder defaults, two-pass, two-pass defaults; one 240-frame encode per stream, preset medium as v1). (b) Holes 1/2/6/7: a fresh long-film test set (`tools/fresh_streams.py`, `experiments/stream_fresh.py`): Elephants Dream and Tears of Steel (v1 train has BBB and Sintel only), 6 contiguous 32-GOP segments per film between 2 % and 85 % of the film (no titles or credits), our own 320x180 clips, curves at preset medium; budgets = segment mean of constant-QP bits per GOP at {24, 30, 36, 42} (the budgets.csv construction) x s. D34 policies and the v1-val-tuned rule are applied unchanged (no training or tuning on this set); streams of N ∈ {8, 16, 32} GOPs, stride N/2; CIs over the 12 segments.
  - Hypotheses, written before any result: **H6** at N = 8, bc_ppo L=2 (the D34 primary) beats the rule on the fresh set with the 95 % CI above 0. **H7** the gain at N = 16 and 32 is not smaller than at N = 8 by more than its CI half-width (policy carries to longer streams). **H8** >1 % over rate within 5 points of the rule. **H9** (encoder RC) bc_ppo L=2 beats every causal encoder config (abr, abr_vbv, abr_tree, abr_aq) on score with the CI above 0.
  - Known limits: 2 films only (12 segments are not independent sources); clips from our downscaler, not P2's (D5 vs his pipeline); same-clip streams only.
  - Outcome (2026-11-12): H6 split (fails on x265, passes on x264), H7–H9 pass. See JOURNAL.
